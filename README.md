## Choosing Matcha for Your Business

When selecting matcha for commercial use, businesses should consider several factors:

**Intended application:** Matcha used for traditional preparation may have different requirements from matcha used in milk-based drinks or baked goods.

**Flavor preferences:** The desired balance of sweetness, umami, and bitterness can vary depending on the recipe.

**Product grade:** Ceremonial, latte-grade, and culinary matcha are intended for different applications.

**Order requirements:** Businesses should consider their expected usage, available packaging options, and wholesale order quantities.

**Branding needs:** Retailers and beverage brands may want to explore private label or custom packaging opportunities.

Matchia's product selection gives businesses options to evaluate according to these requirements.
